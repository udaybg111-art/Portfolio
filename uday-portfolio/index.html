01. ABOUT ME

Precision QA Engineering for High-Stakes Software

I am a dedicated QA Engineer with 1.5+ years of hands-on experience spanning both automated test architecture and thorough manual exploratory verification. My career has been forged in one of the most demanding software domains: Enterprise Industrial Automation & Distributed Control Systems (DCS) at Yokogawa India Limited.

In industrial automation, software reliability is paramount. I've validated platforms like CENTUM VP, ProSafe-RS (Safety Instrumented System), ExaPilot, and PACE, directly interfacing with DCS hardware such as Field Control Stations (FCS) and Human Interface Stations (HIS) to achieve end-to-end verification.

My automation philosophy centers on creating clean, maintainable, and scalable test frameworks using Python, Pytest, Selenium WebDriver, and Playwright. Leveraging the Page Object Model (POM), I eliminate test flakiness, maximize script reusability, and integrate automated regression suites into fast-paced Agile sprint cycles.
